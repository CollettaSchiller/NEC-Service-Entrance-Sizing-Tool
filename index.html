2. Heating, cooling and motors

Enter the electrical load that is actually present. For motors, enter the motor load once; do not also enter that same motor as a separate fixed appliance.

3. Fixed & special appliances

Ranges/cooking appliances and dryers get their own NEC demand treatment. Other permanently connected appliances can receive the 75% demand factor when four or more qualify.

4. 120 V phase assignment

For 120 V fixed loads, choose phase A or B. 240 V line-to-line loads do not add current to the neutral. The lighting/small-appliance/laundry demand is assumed split as evenly as possible for the phase-balance illustration.

Notes & limitations

This calculator is designed for a single-phase 120/240 V dwelling service or the entire load of a dwelling unit. Local amendments, utility requirements, service-entrance installation conditions, conductor temperature ratings, terminal ratings, raceway fill, ambient temperature, voltage drop, fault-current withstand, equipment listings, and AHJ requirements can change the final field selection.
The calculator reports a minimum code-based sizing result; it does not certify an installation, permit, utility service, or engineered design.
